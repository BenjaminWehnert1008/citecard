Get a citation card.

Screenshots of paper headers look different for every journal and are hard to read on a slide. CiteCard gives every paper the same clean card, ready to copy and paste.

Works with a link to the paper, a DOI, or just its title. Several papers? One per line.

It makes way for your own cards.

Metadata and citation counts come from OpenAlex and Crossref, shared first and last authors from PubMed Central and PubMed. Counts differ between databases, so every card names its source and month.

Journal logos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. They are trademarks of their publishers and appear only to identify the journal.
